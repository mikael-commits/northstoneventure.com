Step 04 · Before You Ask

Common Questions Answered

Click any question below for a detailed written answer.

Most agencies build generic systems and hand them off. We build specifically for CPA and tax firms — the workflows, language, and client communication sequences are all designed around how accounting practices operate. We also come from a finance and compliance background, which means we understand the professional standards your firm is held to. We stay involved through implementation, not just setup.

Referrals are the best kind of client — but also the least predictable. You've likely done everything right for a client and still lost them to H&R Block because of a $99 offer. Consumer loyalty in this space is fragile. Our system doesn't replace your referral network — it makes sure you're not 100% dependent on it, and it ensures the clients you've already earned don't quietly leave without you knowing why.

That's exactly why you do it now — so it's running on autopilot before next season. Setup takes 30 days. If you start in April or May, the system is live and tested well before the next busy season begins. The firms getting ahead of this are building the infrastructure during the quiet months, not scrambling to fix problems during their busiest weeks.

Timeline depends on your firm's size, client volume, and the complexity of your build — typically 30–60 days from kickoff to live. The process follows four phases regardless of scope.

Discovery. You share a snapshot of your current tools and key numbers. We map where revenue is leaking and what's recoverable.

Build. We configure everything — AI receptionist, AR automation, lead generation — integrated with your existing software. You review and approve as we go.

Launch. Systems go live with active monitoring. Your team gets a walkthrough so everyone knows what's running.

Optimization. We iterate monthly based on real performance data. You get reports. We handle refinements. We don't disappear after setup.

Your job is to show up, approve, and keep doing what you do best. We'll give you a specific timeline on the call once we understand your firm's scope.

Every firm is different, but based on what we've modeled across CPA and tax practices of varying sizes, ROI typically lands in the 2.5x–6x range — and in some cases higher, depending on your starting point. Here's where that return comes from:

New client acquisition. The lead generation system brings in qualified prospects who are actively looking for a CPA. One new retained client at $2,500–$5,000 per year compounds. Three new clients a month changes your growth trajectory entirely.

Freed capacity for billable and advisory work. When your team isn't managing phone admin, appointment scheduling, and follow-up manually, those hours go back into billable work and higher-value client relationships. At $150–$300 per billable hour, that recovery adds up fast.

Enhanced client experience and retention. 24/7 call coverage, instant responses, proactive communication — clients feel looked after. Firms using these systems report stronger client loyalty and fewer quiet departures to competitors or discount services.

Faster collection on aged receivables. The AR automation sends structured, timely reminders and escalates appropriately — without awkward manual follow-up. Firms typically recover a meaningful portion of their aged balance within the first 60 days.

We'll build a projection specific to your firm on the call — based on your actual client base, billing rate, and current gaps.

Our systems operate at the workflow and communication layer — scheduling, reminders, follow-up, call handling. We don't access or store your clients' tax data. Each build is scoped upfront to make sure it meets IRC 7216 and FTC Safeguards before we touch anything. Automations run inside your own cloud environment, data is encrypted in transit, and we don't reuse data across clients. The enterprise AI models we use don't store or train on your data.

Be at your office, join from a desktop or laptop, and come ready to review your numbers and process live while we share screens.
